Our Story

RE/MAX Rex, originally founded as Realtor Exclusive LLC by Ana Armua, has been a beacon of excellence in the Florida real estate market since 2017. Upon joining the RE/MAX franchise, we adopted the name RE/MAX Rex and embarked on a journey of relentless growth and development. Our company has continuously worked to establish itself among Florida's top real estate firms, evidenced by our expanding team of dedicated agents.
 
 
 

Our Mission

Our mission at RE/MAX Rex is to provide unparalleled real estate services that exceed client expectations. We strive to create a supportive and empowering environment for our agents, enabling them to achieve their professional goals while delivering outstanding results for our clients. Through integrity, dedication, and innovation, we aim to be the preferred real estate company in Florida.
 
 
 

Our Vision

Our vision is to be recognized as the most trusted and respected real estate company in Florida. We aspire to set the standard for excellence in the industry by continually improving our services, expanding our team of skilled agents, and fostering lasting relationships with our clients. We envision a future where RE/MAX Rex is synonymous with quality, reliability, and exceptional customer service.
 
 
 

Our Values

At RE/MAX Rex, our core values guide everything we do. We are committed to:

-Christian Values: Upholding principles of faith, compassion, and integrity in all our interactions.
-Commitment to Clients: Prioritizing the needs and satisfaction of our clients in every transaction.
-Honesty: Ensuring transparency and truthfulness in all our dealings.
-Respect: Valuing and respecting each client, agent, and partner we work with.

Our Values

-Loyalty: Building and maintaining trust through steadfast loyalty to our clients and team.
-Professionalism: Maintaining the highest standards of professionalism in all our services.
-Innovation: Continuously seeking new and better ways to serve our clients and improve our business.
-Community Engagement: Actively participating in and giving back to the communities we serve.

These values form the foundation of RE/MAX Rex and drive our commitment to excellence in the real estate industry.
